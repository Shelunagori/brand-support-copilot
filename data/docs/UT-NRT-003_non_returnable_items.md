# Non-Returnable and Non-Exchangeable Items

Policy-ID: UT-NRT-003
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose

Certain products cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene or commercial reasons. This document defines exactly which items fall in that category, how they are labelled on urbanethreads.in, and how agents should handle requests about them. It works alongside the Returns Policy (UT-RET-001) and the Exchange Policy (UT-EXC-002).

## 2. The rule

The following items have **no return and no exchange**:

1. **Innerwear** - briefs, trunks, boxers, vests, bras, camisoles worn as innerwear, shapewear, thermals worn as a base layer, and lounge shorts sold as innerwear.
2. **Socks** - all socks, including multi-packs, ankle socks, no-show socks and sports socks, opened or unopened.
3. **"Final Sale" items** - any product tagged **Final Sale** on the product page, cart, order confirmation or invoice. This typically includes end-of-season clearance, sample sale and outlet products.

This applies regardless of whether the packet is sealed, whether the item was worn, or how many days have passed since delivery.

## 3. The only exception: damaged or wrong item

If a non-returnable item arrives **damaged, defective, or is the wrong item** (wrong product, wrong size versus order, wrong colour), the customer may raise a claim under UT-DMG-009. The claim must be reported **within 48 hours of delivery** with an unboxing photo or video. If approved, we offer a replacement or a refund. Outside 48 hours, or without unboxing proof, the claim is rejected.

A change of mind, a size that "does not fit" when the correct size was delivered, or a colour that "looks different in person" are not damage and do not qualify.

## 4. How Final Sale is shown

- A red **FINAL SALE** badge on the product tile and product page.
- The line "This item is Final Sale: no returns, no exchanges" above the Add to Cart button.
- The tag repeated on the order confirmation email and on the GST invoice line.

Because the label is shown at multiple points before purchase, agents must not make goodwill exceptions for Final Sale. Requests to override should be politely declined; if the customer becomes abusive or threatens legal action, escalate per UT-ESC-012.

## 5. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Customer bought a 3-pack of socks for ₹399, unopened, wants to return on day 2. Not allowed; socks are non-returnable.
- **Example B:** Customer bought a Final Sale linen shirt for ₹899 and wants a different size on day 5. Not allowed; Final Sale has no exchange either.
- **Example C:** Customer ordered black trunks size M and received white trunks size S; reported 20 hours after delivery with an unboxing video. Valid wrong-item claim under UT-DMG-009; offer replacement or refund.
- **Example D:** Same as C but reported on day 3 (more than 48 hours). Claim rejected as per UT-DMG-009.
- **Example E:** Thermal top sold as outerwear (not innerwear category) - check the category on the product page. If listed under "Winterwear - Outer", the normal 7-day return and 15-day exchange windows apply.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Socks ka pack return karna hai, abhi khola bhi nahi." -> Socks cannot be returned even if unopened.
- "Final sale wali dress ka size exchange ho jayega?" -> No, Final Sale items cannot be returned or exchanged.
- "The boxer briefs are too tight." -> Innerwear is non-returnable for hygiene reasons; suggest the size guide for future orders.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Old FAQ said socks can be exchanged. Is that true?**
A: No. That was an archived 2024 FAQ that has been superseded. Socks are neither returnable nor exchangeable.

**Q: Can I get store credit instead for a Final Sale item?**
A: No. Final Sale items are not eligible for return, exchange or store credit unless damaged or wrong and reported within 48 hours.

**Q: Are watches and sunglasses non-returnable?**
A: No, they follow the normal 7-day return window (UT-RET-001), and also carry a 6-month manufacturing warranty (UT-WAR-007), unless marked Final Sale.

**Q: Is a sealed innerwear pack returnable?**
A: No. Innerwear is non-returnable regardless of packaging.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-NRT-003**. Be empathetic but firm. Never suggest a workaround such as "return it as damaged". If a genuine damage or wrong-item claim exists, route to UT-DMG-009 and check the 48-hour rule.
