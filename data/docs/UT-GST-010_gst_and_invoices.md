# GST and Invoices

Policy-ID: UT-GST-010
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance and Tax, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document explains how Goods and Services Tax (GST) is applied to Urbane Threads products sold on urbanethreads.in, how invoices are issued, and how B2B customers can obtain a GSTIN invoice. Marketplace orders receive invoices from the marketplace (UT-MKT-008).

All prices displayed on urbanethreads.in are **inclusive of GST**.

## 2. GST rates on apparel

GST on apparel depends on the **selling price per piece** (after discount):

- **5% GST** on apparel priced **₹1,000 or less per piece** (i.e. ≤ ₹1,000).
- **12% GST** on apparel priced **above ₹1,000 per piece**.

The slab is decided per piece, not on the total order value. A ₹1,000 shirt is taxed at 5%; a ₹1,001 shirt is taxed at 12%.

Accessories (watches, sunglasses, bags, belts) carry the GST rate applicable to their HSN code, which is shown on the invoice. Agents should not quote accessory rates from memory; refer customers to the invoice.

## 3. Worked GST examples

- **Example A:** One T-shirt at ₹799. Rate 5%. Price is inclusive, so the taxable value is ₹760.95 and GST is ₹38.05.
- **Example B:** One shirt at exactly ₹1,000. Rate **5%** because the price is not above ₹1,000.
- **Example C:** One jacket at ₹2,499. Rate 12%.
- **Example D:** Order of three T-shirts at ₹600 each (total ₹1,800). Each piece is ₹600, so 5% applies to each, even though the order total exceeds ₹1,000.
- **Example E:** A ₹1,200 kurta with a 20% coupon, final price ₹960. Rate is 5% because the post-discount per-piece price is ₹1,000 or less.

## 4. Invoices

- A **GST invoice is emailed** to the registered email address **on dispatch** of the order.
- The invoice can also be downloaded from **My Orders** after dispatch.
- For multi-shipment orders, one invoice is issued per shipment.
- On a return, a credit note is issued and emailed when the refund is initiated.

## 5. B2B invoices with GSTIN

- Business customers can get a B2B invoice showing their company name and **GSTIN** so they can claim input tax credit.
- The GSTIN must be **added at checkout before the order is placed** (Checkout -> "Add GST details").
- **A GSTIN cannot be added to an order after it has been placed.** We cannot reissue or modify an invoice to add a GSTIN later, because invoices are reported to the GST portal as issued.
- If a customer forgot to add the GSTIN, the options are: keep the order with a B2C invoice, or cancel before dispatch (UT-CAN-011) and place a fresh order with the GSTIN.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Invoice kab milega?" -> Emailed on dispatch; also downloadable from My Orders.
- "GST number add karna bhool gaya, ab add kar do." -> Not possible after order placement.
- "₹1,000 wali shirt pe kitna GST hai?" -> 5%.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Why was I charged 12% GST on one item and 5% on another?**
A: Items priced above ₹1,000 per piece attract 12%; items ₹1,000 or below attract 5%.

**Q: Is GST added at checkout on top of the price?**
A: No, prices shown are GST-inclusive.

**Q: Can you change the billing name on my invoice?**
A: Billing details are taken from checkout. Invoices cannot be modified after issue; for anything beyond this, escalate per UT-ESC-012.

**Q: Can I get a GST invoice for my Flipkart order from you?**
A: Please download it from Flipkart.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-GST-010**. Apply the per-piece rule carefully, especially at exactly ₹1,000 (5%). Never promise to add a GSTIN after the order is placed. Do not give tax advice beyond this policy.
