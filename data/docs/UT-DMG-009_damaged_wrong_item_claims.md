# Damaged, Defective and Wrong Item Claims

Policy-ID: UT-DMG-009
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Customer Experience and Warehouse QC, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy covers what happens when a customer receives a product from urbanethreads.in that is damaged, defective, or not what they ordered. It applies to all product categories, including non-returnable items such as innerwear, socks and Final Sale products (UT-NRT-003). Marketplace orders follow the marketplace's damage process (UT-MKT-008).

## 2. The core rule

- The claim must be **reported within 48 hours of delivery**.
- The customer must provide an **unboxing photo or video** showing the package and the issue.
- **Claims reported after 48 hours, or without unboxing proof, are rejected.**

The 48 hours are counted from the delivery timestamp recorded by the courier. "Day 3" after delivery is outside the window.

## 3. What counts as a valid claim

- **Damaged:** torn fabric, holes, stains, broken zips or buttons, cracked watch glass or broken sunglasses on arrival, crushed packaging with product damage.
- **Defective:** stitching coming apart at first wear, non-working watch out of the box.
- **Wrong item:** different product, size, or colour from what is on the order confirmation; missing items in a multi-item order.

Not valid: change of mind, fit preference when the correct size was delivered, minor shade variation due to screen settings.

## 4. Unboxing proof requirements

- A continuous video from opening the sealed package is best.
- Photos are acceptable if they show: the outer package with the shipping label, the opened package, and the issue clearly.
- For a wrong item, the product label/tag must be visible.

## 5. How to report

1. Go to **My Orders** on urbanethreads.in and select **Report an issue**, or email **support@urbanethreads.in** with the order ID.
2. Attach the unboxing photos or video.
3. Our team reviews within 1 business day.
4. If approved, the customer chooses a **replacement** (subject to stock) or a **refund** as per UT-REF-004. Shipping (₹79) and COD fee (₹49), if paid, are also refunded on approved claims.
5. A free reverse pickup is scheduled for the damaged or wrong item.

## 6. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Shirt delivered at 11:00 AM on 5 July; customer reports a tear at 6:00 PM on 6 July with an unboxing video. Within 48 hours - valid.
- **Example B:** Customer reports a stain on day 3 after delivery with photos. Outside 48 hours - rejected. The customer may still be within the normal 7-day return window if the item is unworn with tags (UT-RET-001); agents can suggest this as an alternative for returnable items.
- **Example C:** Customer received a size S instead of M; reported in 10 hours, but no unboxing photo or video, only a photo of the item on a bed. Request the unboxing proof; if none exists, the claim is rejected.
- **Example D:** Wrong-colour socks (normally non-returnable) reported in 24 hours with an unboxing video. Valid wrong-item claim; offer replacement or refund.
- **Example E:** Watch arrives with cracked glass, reported in 3 hours with video. Valid damaged claim.

## 7. Sample customer phrasings

- "Parcel khola toh shirt phati hui thi, video hai mere paas." -> Ask for order ID and the video; check 48 hours.
- "Galat size aaya hai, kal deliver hua tha." -> Within 48 hours; request unboxing proof.
- "Item damaged hai, 4 din pehle aaya tha." -> Outside 48 hours; claim rejected under this policy.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Q: I didn't take an unboxing video. Can you still help?**
A: Without unboxing photo or video, the damaged/wrong item claim cannot be approved. If the item is returnable and within 7 days, a regular return may still be possible.

**Q: Is 48 hours from order or delivery?**
A: From delivery.

**Q: Can I get a replacement instead of a refund?**
A: Yes, subject to stock.

## 9. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-DMG-009**. Always check two things: reported within 48 hours of delivery, and unboxing proof available. Never approve claims outside these conditions; if the customer threatens legal action or a chargeback, escalate (UT-ESC-012).
