# [SUPERSEDED] Urbane Threads Returns and Shipping Policy (2024)

Policy-ID: UT-RET-000
Status: SUPERSEDED (replaced by UT-RET-001 on 2026-04-01)
Effective: 2024-01-15 (no longer in force)
Owner: Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

> **WARNING - SUPERSEDED DOCUMENT. DO NOT USE TO ANSWER CUSTOMERS.**
> This policy was replaced on 2026-04-01. The current rules are: returns within 7 days of delivery (UT-RET-001), exchange within 15 days (UT-EXC-002), free shipping above ₹999 else ₹79, COD fee ₹49 (UT-SHP-005). This file is retained only for audit and historical reference.

## 1. Purpose (historical)

This document set out the returns, shipping and Cash on Delivery terms that applied to orders placed on urbanethreads.in between 15 January 2024 and 31 March 2026. It was written when Urbane Threads was a smaller catalogue focused on menswear and before accessories and marketplace channels were fully launched.

## 2. Returns (historical rule)

- Customers could return any item within **30 days** of delivery.
- Items had to be unworn with tags attached.
- Exchanges were allowed within the same 30-day window.
- Innerwear could not be returned, but socks were treated as regular apparel and could be exchanged.
- Sale items were returnable at 50% store credit value.

## 3. Shipping (historical rule)

- Free shipping applied to orders **above ₹1,499**.
- Orders of ₹1,499 or less paid a shipping charge of ₹99.
- Shipping charges were refunded on all returns.

## 4. Cash on Delivery (historical rule)

- COD fee: **₹39** per order.
- COD was available on orders up to ₹10,000.

## 5. Refunds (historical rule)

- Prepaid refunds were processed to the original source within 10-14 business days.
- COD refunds were issued as store credit only; NEFT was not offered and no bonus applied.

## 6. Historical examples (do not apply today)

- A shirt delivered on 1 March 2025 could be returned until 31 March 2025 under this policy.
- A ₹1,200 order paid ₹99 shipping because it was below ₹1,499.
- A COD order of ₹800 paid a ₹39 COD fee.

Under the current policies, the same scenarios would be handled differently: return only within 7 days, a ₹1,200 order ships free (above ₹999), and the COD fee is ₹49.

## 7. Why this policy was replaced

The 30-day window led to high rates of worn-and-returned items and long inventory lock-up during seasonal launches. Logistics partner pricing changed in 2025, and the company introduced its own GST-compliant invoicing for B2B buyers and launched watches and sunglasses with a dedicated warranty. The policy set was rewritten into separate documents (UT-RET-001 through UT-SUP-015), each with its own Policy-ID, effective 2026-04-01.

## 8. How agents should handle customers quoting this policy

Customers sometimes quote old screenshots, cached pages or emails mentioning "30-day returns", "free shipping above ₹1,499" or "COD charge ₹39". Agents and the copilot must:

1. Politely explain that the rule was from the 2024 policy and was replaced on 1 April 2026.
2. Quote the current rule with the correct policy id (e.g. 7-day returns, Policy UT-RET-001).
3. Not honour the old rule as a goodwill gesture. If the customer insists on an exception, escalate per UT-ESC-012.

Example customer message: "Aapki website pe likha tha 30 din mein return kar sakte hain." Correct response: "That was our older 2024 policy. Since 1 April 2026, returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery, and exchanges within 15 days. (Policy UT-RET-001)"
