> **ARCHIVED AND SUPERSEDED. This FAQ contains outdated information and must NOT be used to answer customers.** It has been replaced by the active policy set UT-RET-001 to UT-SUP-015 effective 2026-04-01. Where this FAQ conflicts with an ACTIVE policy, the ACTIVE policy always wins.

## About this archive

This FAQ was published on the urbanethreads.in help page in March 2024 and was updated informally through 2025. It was taken down when the 2026 policy set went live. It is kept here so that support agents can recognise outdated claims customers may quote from screenshots or saved pages.

## Returns and exchanges (OUTDATED)

**Q: How long do I have to return an item?**
A: You can return any item within 30 days of delivery, no questions asked.
*(Outdated. Current rule: 7 days from delivery - UT-RET-001.)*

**Q: Can I exchange socks if they don't fit?**
A: Yes, socks can be exchanged for a different size within 30 days.
*(Outdated. Current rule: socks cannot be returned or exchanged - UT-NRT-003.)*

**Q: Can I return a sale item?**
A: Yes, sale items can be returned for store credit.
*(Outdated. Current rule: Final Sale items have no return and no exchange.)*

**Q: Is reverse pickup free?**
A: Yes, and we also refund the original shipping charge.
*(Partly outdated. Original shipping is not refunded on voluntary returns today.)*

## Refunds (OUTDATED)

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: Refunds are processed in 10-14 business days.
*(Outdated. Current rule: 5-7 business days for prepaid - UT-REF-004.)*

**Q: How do I get a refund on a COD order?**
A: COD refunds are issued as store credit only.
*(Outdated. Current rule: NEFT bank transfer or store credit with a 5% bonus.)*

## Shipping and COD (OUTDATED)

**Q: Is shipping free?**
A: Shipping is free on orders above ₹1,499. Otherwise a ₹99 charge applies.
*(Outdated. Current rule: free above ₹999, else ₹79 - UT-SHP-005.)*

**Q: What is the COD fee?**
A: ₹39 per order.
*(Outdated. Current fee: ₹49.)*

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: 3-5 days anywhere in India.
*(Outdated. Current: Metro 2-4 days, non-metro 5-7 days, North-East/J&K 7-10 days - UT-DLV-006.)*

## Warranty (OUTDATED)

**Q: Do watches have a warranty?**
A: Yes, all watches have a 1-year warranty, including against water damage for water-resistant models.
*(Outdated. Current: 6-month manufacturing warranty; no warranty on physical or water damage - UT-WAR-007.)*

## Damaged items (OUTDATED)

**Q: I received a damaged item. When should I report it?**
A: Report it any time within 7 days. Photos are helpful but not required.
*(Outdated. Current: within 48 hours of delivery with unboxing photo/video - UT-DMG-009.)*

## Marketplace orders (OUTDATED)

**Q: I bought from Amazon. Can Urbane Threads refund me?**
A: Yes, email us and we can help process your refund.
*(Outdated and incorrect. Current: marketplace orders follow Amazon/Flipkart processes; brand support cannot refund them - UT-MKT-008.)*

## GST (OUTDATED)

**Q: Can I add my GSTIN after ordering?**
A: Yes, email us within 7 days and we will reissue the invoice.
*(Outdated. Current: GSTIN must be added before the order is placed; it cannot be added after - UT-GST-010.)*
