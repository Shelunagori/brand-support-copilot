# Escalation to a Human Agent

Policy-ID: UT-ESC-012
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Customer Experience Leadership, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose

The Urbane Threads support copilot and first-line agents resolve most questions directly using policy. Some situations, however, carry legal, financial or reputational risk, or are not covered by any policy. This document defines when a conversation **must** be escalated to a human senior agent, and how the copilot or agent should respond while escalating.

## 2. Mandatory escalation triggers

Escalate to a human in **all** of the following cases:

1. **Legal threats** - the customer mentions a legal notice, consumer court, consumer forum, lawyer, advocate, FIR, police complaint or suing the company.
2. **Chargebacks or payment disputes** - the customer says they have raised, or will raise, a chargeback or dispute with their bank or card issuer.
3. **High-value orders** - any order with a total value **above ₹25,000**.
4. **Abusive messages** - profanity, slurs, threats of harm, or harassment directed at staff.
5. **Anything not covered by policy** - requests for exceptions outside written policy (e.g. return after 7 days, exchange after 15 days, warranty extension, compensation not in policy), questions about topics no active policy covers, or conflicting information the agent cannot resolve.

When in doubt, escalate. It is always better to escalate than to invent a policy.

## 3. What the copilot should say when escalating

- Acknowledge the concern in one sentence.
- State that a senior support specialist will review the case.
- Give the expected response time: **within 24 hours** (business days).
- Do **not** admit liability, promise an outcome, offer compensation, or argue.
- Do not continue arguing policy after the escalation is made.
- For abusive messages, stay calm and professional; do not mirror the language.

**Template:** "I understand this is frustrating. I've escalated your case to a senior support specialist, who will contact you within 24 hours at your registered email. (Policy UT-ESC-012)"

## 4. Worked examples

- **Example A (legal):** "If my refund doesn't come by Friday I'm sending a legal notice." -> Escalate. Do not promise the refund date.
- **Example B (chargeback):** "I've already filed a chargeback with HDFC." -> Escalate.
- **Example C (high value):** Customer asks about returning a ₹32,000 order of three watches and a jacket. Order value is above ₹25,000 -> escalate, even though the return window question itself is simple.
- **Example D (abusive):** Customer uses profanity calling the team "chor" and threatening staff. -> Escalate, respond politely.
- **Example E (not covered):** "Can you deliver to my cousin in Dubai?" or "Can I return this after 25 days as a special case?" -> Not covered by policy / out-of-policy exception -> escalate.
- **Example F (not escalation):** "What is your return window?" -> Answer directly: 7 days (UT-RET-001).

## 5. Hinglish triggers to recognise

- "Consumer court mein case karunga." -> Legal threat.
- "Bank mein chargeback daal diya hai." -> Chargeback.
- "Tum log fraud ho, gaali ..." -> Abusive.
- "Lawyer se baat kar raha hoon." -> Legal threat.

## 6. Marketplace orders and escalation

For Amazon or Flipkart orders, the default is to redirect to the marketplace (UT-MKT-008). However, if the customer makes a legal threat or is abusive, escalate as well, while still stating that refunds for marketplace orders are handled by the marketplace.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Should the copilot still answer the policy question while escalating?**
A: It may state the relevant policy briefly and neutrally, but must not promise any outcome. The human agent will decide.

**Q: Is an order of exactly ₹25,000 escalated?**
A: The mandatory threshold is orders above ₹25,000. Orders at or below that follow normal handling unless another trigger applies.

**Q: What if the customer just sounds angry but is not abusive?**
A: Respond with empathy and resolve per policy. Escalate only if a trigger applies.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-ESC-012** whenever escalating, and set the escalate flag in the copilot response. Keep the message short, calm and free of promises.
