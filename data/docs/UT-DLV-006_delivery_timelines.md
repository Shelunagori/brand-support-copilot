# Delivery Timelines

Policy-ID: UT-DLV-006
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Operations and Logistics, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the standard delivery timelines for orders placed on urbanethreads.in and shipped within India. It also explains how timelines are counted and what agents should say when an order is delayed. Amazon and Flipkart orders are delivered by the marketplace and follow the timelines shown on those platforms (UT-MKT-008).

## 2. Standard delivery timelines

Timelines are counted in **business days from dispatch** (not from order placement). Orders are usually dispatched within 1 business day of placement if placed before 2 PM.

| Region | Delivery time after dispatch |
|---|---|
| **Metro cities** (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) | **2-4 days** |
| **Non-metro** (all other serviceable pincodes in mainland India) | **5-7 days** |
| **North-East states and Jammu & Kashmir** (including Ladakh) | **7-10 days** |

North-East covers Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura. Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep are serviceable only for prepaid orders and follow the 7-10 day timeline on a best-effort basis.

## 3. What can affect timelines

- Public holidays, strikes, extreme weather, elections and regional restrictions.
- Incorrect or incomplete address or phone number.
- Festive peaks (Diwali, End of Season Sale) may add 1-2 days; this is shown on the product page during the sale.
- Exchanges: the replacement follows the same timelines from its dispatch date.

## 4. Delayed orders

An order is considered **delayed** when it has not been delivered by the last day of its timeline window. For a delayed order, agents should:

1. Share the latest tracking status (see UT-ORD-013).
2. Raise a priority ticket with the courier partner.
3. Offer cancellation if the order is not yet out for delivery and the customer prefers; for a dispatched order, the customer can refuse delivery (UT-CAN-011) and the refund follows UT-REF-004.

Agents must not promise a specific delivery date or time slot that the courier has not confirmed, and must not offer compensation that is not in policy. Requests for compensation beyond policy are escalated (UT-ESC-012).

## 5. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Order to Pune dispatched Monday. Expected delivery Wednesday to Friday (2-4 days).
- **Example B:** Order to Indore (non-metro) dispatched Tuesday. Expected within 5-7 business days.
- **Example C:** Order to Srinagar (J&K) dispatched on the 3rd. Expected within 7-10 business days, so by about the 15th considering Sundays.
- **Example D:** Order to Guwahati placed but not delivered after 8 days from dispatch. Still within the 7-10 day North-East window; it is not yet delayed. Share tracking and reassure.
- **Example E:** Order to Mumbai, 6 business days after dispatch, not delivered. This is delayed (beyond 2-4 days). Raise courier escalation and share an update within 24 hours.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Mera order kab tak aayega? Main Jaipur se hoon." -> Non-metro, 5-7 days from dispatch.
- "Srinagar mein delivery kitne din mein hogi?" -> J&K: 7-10 days.
- "Order 5 din se ship hua hai, Delhi mein hai, abhi tak nahi aaya." -> Metro 2-4 days, so delayed; escalate with courier.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do you offer same-day or next-day delivery?**
A: Not currently. Standard timelines apply.

**Q: Is delivery time counted from when I ordered?**
A: From dispatch. Most orders dispatch within 1 business day.

**Q: Do you deliver on Sundays?**
A: Some courier partners deliver on Sundays in metros, but Sundays are not counted as business days for timelines.

**Q: Do you deliver outside India?**
A: No, Urbane Threads delivers only within India.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-DLV-006**. State the correct band: Metro 2-4 days, non-metro 5-7 days, North-East/J&K 7-10 days. Do not promise guaranteed dates. For shipping charges refer to UT-SHP-005; for tracking and address changes refer to UT-ORD-013.
