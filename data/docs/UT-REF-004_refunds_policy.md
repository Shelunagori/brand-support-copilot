# Urbane Threads Refunds Policy

Policy-ID: UT-REF-004
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document explains how and when customers receive refunds for orders placed on urbanethreads.in. Refunds are triggered by an approved return (UT-RET-001), a cancellation before dispatch (UT-CAN-011), or an approved damaged/wrong item claim (UT-DMG-009). Refunds for Amazon and Flipkart orders are issued only by the marketplace; Urbane Threads support cannot refund marketplace orders (UT-MKT-008).

## 2. Refund method depends on how the customer paid

### 2.1 Prepaid orders (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets)

- Refund goes back to the **original payment source** only.
- Timeline: **5-7 business days** after the refund is initiated.
- We cannot redirect a prepaid refund to a different bank account, UPI ID or card.
- Business days exclude Sundays and national bank holidays.

### 2.2 Cash on Delivery (COD) orders

COD customers choose one of two options:

1. **Bank transfer (NEFT)** to an Indian bank account in the customer's name. The customer provides account number, IFSC and account holder name in the return form. NEFT is processed within 5-7 business days after initiation.
2. **Store credit** in the customer's Urbane Threads wallet, with a **5% bonus** on top of the refund amount. Store credit is issued within 24 hours of QC pass. See UT-PAY-014 for store credit rules.

We do not refund COD orders in cash, and we do not refund to a third party's bank account.

## 3. When the refund is initiated

- **Return:** within 48 hours of the item passing quality check at our warehouse.
- **Cancellation before dispatch:** immediately on cancellation.
- **Damaged/wrong item:** after the claim is approved and the item is picked up (or immediately if pickup is waived).

## 4. What is refunded

- Product price paid (after discounts and coupons).
- GST charged on the product.
- Shipping charge (₹79) and COD fee (₹49) are refunded **only** for cancellations before dispatch and approved damaged/wrong item claims. They are not refunded for voluntary returns.

## 5. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Prepaid UPI order of ₹1,850 returned. QC passed on Monday. Refund initiated Monday; customer should see ₹1,850 in their UPI-linked bank account within 5-7 business days.
- **Example B:** COD order of ₹1,200 returned; customer chooses store credit. Store credit = ₹1,200 + 5% bonus (₹60) = **₹1,260**.
- **Example C:** COD order of ₹2,000 returned; customer chooses NEFT. ₹2,000 transferred to their bank account in 5-7 business days. No bonus applies to NEFT.
- **Example D:** COD order of ₹780 (₹652 product + ₹79 shipping + ₹49 COD fee) returned voluntarily. Refund = ₹652 only (shipping and COD fee not refunded).
- **Example E:** Customer says "refund mere dusre account mein bhej do" for a prepaid card payment. Not possible; prepaid refunds go only to the original payment source.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Refund kab aayega?" -> Prepaid: 5-7 business days to original source; COD: NEFT 5-7 business days, or store credit with 5% bonus within 24 hours.
- "Mera paisa 10 din se nahi aaya." -> Check initiation date; if more than 7 business days since initiation, share the bank reference number (ARN/UTR) and ask the customer to check with their bank. If still unresolved, raise an internal ticket.
- "I'll do a chargeback if I don't get my money." -> Escalate to a human agent (UT-ESC-012).

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Old FAQ said refunds take 10-14 days. Which is right?**
A: The current timeline is 5-7 business days. The 10-14 day figure was from an archived 2024 FAQ.

**Q: Can I get cash back for a COD order?**
A: No. Choose NEFT bank transfer or store credit (with 5% bonus).

**Q: Can I convert store credit back to my bank account later?**
A: No. Once store credit is issued, it cannot be converted to cash.

**Q: Will you refund my Amazon order?**
A: No. Amazon processes refunds for Amazon orders. Please raise the request in your Amazon account.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-REF-004**. Never promise a refund faster than the stated timelines, never promise refunds for marketplace orders, and escalate any chargeback or dispute mention (UT-ESC-012).
