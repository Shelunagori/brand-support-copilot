# Urbane Threads Exchange Policy

Policy-ID: UT-EXC-002
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how customers can exchange an item bought on urbanethreads.in for a different size, colour, or another product. It applies only to orders placed directly with Urbane Threads. Orders from Amazon and Flipkart follow the marketplace process (UT-MKT-008); our team cannot process exchanges for those orders.

## 2. The core rule

- **Exchange window: 15 days from the date of delivery.**
- The item must be unworn, unwashed and have all original tags attached.
- Innerwear, socks and "Final Sale" items are **not eligible** for exchange (UT-NRT-003).
- Each order line can be exchanged once. A second exchange on an already-exchanged item is not allowed; the customer can return it within 7 days of the exchange delivery if eligible under UT-RET-001.

Note the difference: **returns are 7 days** (UT-RET-001) and **exchanges are 15 days** from delivery. Agents should mention exchange when a customer is too late for a return but still within 15 days.

## 3. Types of exchange

1. **Size exchange (same product):** free of cost. Reverse pickup and forward shipment are both free.
2. **Colour exchange (same product, same price):** free of cost, subject to stock.
3. **Different product:** the customer can exchange for a different product. If the new product costs more, the customer pays the difference online (COD is not available for the difference). If it costs less, the difference is issued as store credit.

## 4. How to raise an exchange

1. Go to **My Orders** on urbanethreads.in and tap **Exchange** on the item.
2. Pick the new size, colour or product.
3. Reverse pickup is scheduled; the replacement is dispatched once the original item is picked up (or, for trusted customers with 3+ completed orders, at the same time as pickup).
4. Delivery timelines for the replacement follow UT-DLV-006 (Metro 2-4 days, non-metro 5-7 days, North-East/J&K 7-10 days).

## 5. Out of stock

If the requested size or colour is out of stock, the customer can choose a different product, wait for restock (we hold the request for up to 7 days), or convert to a return if still within the 7-day return window. If the 7-day return window has passed, the value is issued as store credit.

## 6. Worked examples

- **Example A:** T-shirt (₹699) delivered on 3 March. On 14 March (day 11) the customer wants size L instead of M. Return window is over, but exchange is allowed until day 15. Size exchange is processed free.
- **Example B:** Shirt (₹1,299) exchanged for a blazer (₹2,999). Customer pays ₹1,700 difference online.
- **Example C:** Chinos (₹1,799) exchanged for a polo (₹1,199). ₹600 is issued as store credit.
- **Example D:** Customer asks to exchange a **Final Sale** dress for another size. Not allowed. Final Sale items cannot be returned or exchanged (UT-NRT-003).
- **Example E:** Delivered 20 days ago. Both return (7 days) and exchange (15 days) windows have closed. Politely decline; do not promise an exception. If the customer insists on an out-of-policy exception, escalate per UT-ESC-012.

## 7. Sample customer phrasings

- "Size badalna hai, M ki jagah L chahiye." -> Size exchange within 15 days.
- "Can I swap this for a different colour?" -> Colour exchange, subject to stock.
- "Exchange kar sakte ho? 12 din ho gaye delivery ko." -> Yes, still within 15 days, if unworn with tags.
- "Socks ka size galat hai, exchange karo." -> Socks are non-exchangeable unless they arrived wrong/damaged and were reported within 48 hours (UT-DMG-009).

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do I pay shipping for an exchange?**
A: No, size and colour exchanges are free for website orders.

**Q: Can I exchange a product I bought on Flipkart?**
A: No, please raise the exchange or return on Flipkart directly (UT-MKT-008).

**Q: Is the exchange window from the order date?**
A: No, 15 days from the delivery date.

**Q: Can I exchange socks or innerwear?**
A: No. Innerwear, socks and Final Sale items have no return and no exchange.

## 9. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-EXC-002** for exchange answers. Always distinguish the 15-day exchange window from the 7-day return window. Never offer exchanges on non-returnable categories. Keep answers short and action-oriented.
