# Shipping Charges and Cash on Delivery (COD)

Policy-ID: UT-SHP-005
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Operations, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)
Replaces: shipping sections of UT-RET-000 (SUPERSEDED)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines shipping charges and Cash on Delivery (COD) rules for orders placed on urbanethreads.in. Urbane Threads ships only within India. Marketplace orders (Amazon, Flipkart) follow the marketplace's shipping rules (UT-MKT-008).

## 2. Shipping charges

- **Free shipping on orders above ₹999** (order value after discounts and coupons, before COD fee).
- **Orders of ₹999 or below: flat shipping charge of ₹79.**
- The threshold is strictly "above ₹999". An order of exactly ₹999 pays ₹79 shipping. An order of ₹1,000 ships free.
- The shipping charge is per order, not per item.

The old threshold of ₹1,499 (2024 policy UT-RET-000) is no longer valid.

## 3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

- **COD fee: ₹49 per order**, charged in addition to any shipping charge.
- **COD is not available on orders above ₹15,000.** Such orders must be prepaid (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets).
- COD is available only in serviceable pincodes; the checkout page shows availability after the pincode is entered.
- COD customers must pay the exact amount in cash or by UPI to the delivery partner at the doorstep.
- COD may be disabled for an account with two or more refused COD deliveries in the past 6 months.

The old COD fee of ₹39 (2024 policy) is no longer valid.

## 5. Worked scenarios

- **Scenario A:** Customer has ₹949 in cart and asks how to avoid shipping. Explain that orders above ₹999 ship free; adding any item that takes the cart above ₹999 removes the ₹79 charge.
- **Scenario B:** Customer wants COD for a ₹16,000 order (e.g., two premium watches). COD is not available above ₹15,000; the customer must pay online, or split into separate orders each within limits if genuinely separate purchases. Agents should not suggest splitting just to bypass the limit; simply state the rule.
- **Scenario C:** Coupon reduces a ₹1,100 cart to ₹950. Shipping of ₹79 applies because the post-discount value is not above ₹999.
- **Scenario D:** Customer asks whether the ₹49 COD fee is refunded on return. Not for voluntary returns; it is refunded only for cancellations before dispatch and approved damaged/wrong item claims (UT-REF-004).

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Free delivery kitne se upar hai?" -> Orders above ₹999 ship free; otherwise ₹79.
- "COD charge kitna hai?" -> ₹49 per order.
- "16 hazaar ka order hai, COD milega?" -> No, COD is not available on orders above ₹15,000.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Your old site said free shipping above ₹1,499.**
A: That was the 2024 policy, superseded on 2026-04-01. Free shipping now applies above ₹999.

**Q: Is GST included in the shipping charge?**
A: Yes, ₹79 is inclusive of applicable taxes.

**Q: Do exchanges have shipping charges?**
A: No, size and colour exchanges are free (UT-EXC-002).

**Q: Is there express shipping?**
A: Not currently. Standard delivery timelines are in UT-DLV-006.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: No, Urbane Threads ships only within India.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-SHP-005**. Always use the exact figures: free above ₹999, else ₹79; COD fee ₹49; no COD above ₹15,000. Never quote ₹1,499 or ₹39.
