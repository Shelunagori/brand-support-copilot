> **NOT A POLICY.** This is a marketing and blog writing guide. It contains promotional language and expired campaign details. It must not be used to answer customer questions about returns, refunds, shipping, warranty or any other policy. Support agents and the copilot must use ACTIVE policies (UT-RET-001 to UT-SUP-015) instead.

## 1. Who this guide is for

This guide helps content writers, social media managers and agency partners write blog posts, Instagram captions, email newsletters and product descriptions in the Urbane Threads voice. It covers brand personality, vocabulary, formatting and a few examples of past campaigns.

## 2. Brand personality

Urbane Threads is "everyday style, made in India, made easy". Our voice is:

- **Confident, not loud.** We let the product speak.
- **Friendly and urban.** We write like a well-dressed friend, not a salesperson.
- **Inclusive.** Sizes XS to 4XL, every body, every city.
- **Rooted.** We celebrate Indian textiles - khadi, linen, handloom cotton - without being preachy.

## 3. Vocabulary we like

- "Easy returns" and "hassle-free exchanges" in banners and captions (keep it light; do not state day counts in marketing copy - link to the help centre instead).
- "Wardrobe heroes", "office-to-chai", "monsoon-ready", "festive edit".
- "Made for Indian summers."

Words to avoid: "cheap", "luxury", "guaranteed", "lifetime", "100% refund anytime".

## 4. Formatting rules

- Headlines in sentence case.
- Use the ₹ symbol, not "Rs." or "INR".
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines) for mobile readers.
- One call to action per post.
- Hashtags: #UrbaneThreads #WearUrbane, maximum five per post.

- "Festive dressing for every budget: under ₹999, under ₹1,999, under ₹4,999."

## 7. Product description template

1. One-line hook.
2. Fabric and fit.
3. Styling tip.
4. Care instructions.
5. Link: "See our returns and exchange policy" (link to the help centre; never paraphrase the policy in the description).

## 8. Legal and compliance notes for writers

- Do not promise delivery dates in marketing copy.
- Do not state return windows, warranty periods or refund timelines in creative copy; these change and live in the policy documents.
- Do not use competitor names.
- Any promotional claim must have campaign dates and be approved by the legal team.

## 9. Note for support

If a customer quotes "easy returns" or "20-day returns" from a blog post or old Diwali banner, explain that marketing copy is not a policy and that the Diwali 2025 extended window has expired. The current return window is 7 days from delivery (UT-RET-001) and exchange window 15 days (UT-EXC-002).
