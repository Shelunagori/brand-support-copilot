# Watches and Sunglasses Warranty

Policy-ID: UT-WAR-007
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Accessories Category and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

Urbane Threads sells its own-label watches and sunglasses. This policy explains the manufacturing warranty on these accessories. It applies to products bought on urbanethreads.in. For watches and sunglasses bought on Amazon or Flipkart, the warranty terms are the same, but the claim must first be raised through the marketplace order page (UT-MKT-008); our team may assist with technical assessment but cannot refund marketplace orders.

## 2. The warranty

- **Duration: 6 months from the date of delivery.**
- **Coverage: manufacturing defects only.**
- The warranty is a **repair or replacement** warranty. If the same model is unavailable, we offer a replacement of equal value or store credit for the price paid.

### 2.1 What is covered (examples)

- Watch movement stops or loses/gains time beyond normal tolerance with a fresh battery.
- Crown, pusher or strap pin failure under normal use.
- Dial or hands coming loose without impact.
- Sunglasses hinge failure, frame joint breaking under normal folding, lens coating peeling without scratching.

### 2.2 What is NOT covered

- **Physical damage:** cracked glass, dented case, bent or snapped frames, scratched lenses, damage from drops or impact.
- **Water damage:** fogging, moisture inside the dial, corroded movement - even on watches marked "water resistant". Water resistance ratings refer to splash resistance, not swimming or bathing.
- Normal wear and tear: strap wear, fading, scratches from daily use.
- Battery replacement (a consumable), loss or theft.
- Repairs or opening of the product by a third party.

## 3. How to make a warranty claim

1. Email **support@urbanethreads.in** with the order ID, a description of the issue, and clear photos or a short video.
2. Our team assesses the issue within 3 business days.
3. If covered, a free reverse pickup is arranged. Repair or replacement is completed within 10-15 business days of receipt.
4. If not covered (physical or water damage), we inform the customer with the reason. A paid repair may be offered where possible.

Proof of purchase (order ID or GST invoice) is mandatory.

## 4. Relationship with returns

- Within **7 days** of delivery, an unused watch or pair of sunglasses with tags can be returned normally under UT-RET-001.
- A manufacturing defect noticed at unboxing should be reported within **48 hours** as a damaged item claim (UT-DMG-009).
- After that, defects are handled under this 6-month warranty.

## 5. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Watch bought for ₹3,499 stops working after 4 months; battery is fine. Covered. Repair or replace.
- **Example B:** Watch shows fog under the glass after the customer wore it while swimming. Water damage - not covered.
- **Example C:** Sunglasses (₹1,999) hinge breaks after 2 months of normal use. Covered.
- **Example D:** Sunglasses dropped and the lens cracked. Physical damage - not covered.
- **Example E:** Watch stops after 8 months. Outside the 6-month warranty - not covered. Offer paid repair if available.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Ghadi mein paani chala gaya, warranty mein theek hoga?" -> No, water damage is not covered.
- "Watch ki warranty kitni hai?" -> 6 months manufacturing warranty.
- "My sunglasses frame snapped when I sat on them." -> Physical damage, not covered.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: The old FAQ said 1 year warranty.**
A: That archived 2024 FAQ is superseded. The current warranty is 6 months.

**Q: Is there warranty on apparel?**
A: No. Apparel issues are handled through returns, exchanges or damaged item claims.

**Q: Will I get a refund under warranty?**
A: Warranty provides repair or replacement, or store credit if the model is unavailable. It does not provide a cash refund.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-WAR-007**. Always say "6-month manufacturing warranty" and clearly state that physical and water damage are excluded. Never extend the warranty period or promise free repair for excluded damage.
