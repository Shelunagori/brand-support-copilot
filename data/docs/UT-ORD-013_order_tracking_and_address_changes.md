# Order Tracking and Address Changes

Policy-ID: UT-ORD-013
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Operations and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy covers how customers can track orders placed on urbanethreads.in and when a delivery address or phone number can be changed. Amazon and Flipkart orders are tracked and managed on the marketplace (UT-MKT-008).

## 2. Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Order placed and payment verified (or COD confirmed). Can be cancelled free. |
| **Packed** | Order packed at warehouse, not yet handed to courier. Can still be cancelled free. |
| **Shipped** | Handed to courier with a tracking number (AWB). Cannot be cancelled (UT-CAN-011). |
| **Out for delivery** | With the delivery agent today. |
| **Delivered** | Delivered; return window (7 days) and exchange window (15 days) start from this date. |
| **RTO** | Returned to origin after failed delivery or refusal. |

## 3. How to track an order

- Log in at urbanethreads.in -> **My Orders** -> select the order -> **Track**.
- A tracking link is sent by SMS, WhatsApp and email once the order is shipped.
- Customers without an account can use **Track Order** on the website with the order ID (starting with UT) and registered phone number.

Delivery timelines are defined in UT-DLV-006 (Metro 2-4 days, non-metro 5-7 days, North-East/J&K 7-10 days, from dispatch).

## 4. Address changes

- **Before dispatch (Confirmed or Packed):** the address can be changed **once**, within the **same city and pincode serviceability**, from My Orders -> Edit Address, or by emailing support@urbanethreads.in. A change to a different city requires cancelling and placing a new order.
- **After dispatch (Shipped):** the address **cannot** be changed. The customer can ask the courier to reattempt delivery, or refuse the delivery and reorder (UT-CAN-011).
- Phone number for delivery can be updated before dispatch.

## 5. Failed delivery attempts

- The courier makes up to **3 delivery attempts**.
- After 3 failed attempts, the order is marked RTO and returned to our warehouse.
- For prepaid RTO orders, the product amount is refunded to the original payment source in 5-7 business days after the package reaches the warehouse (UT-REF-004). The shipping charge (₹79) and COD fee (₹49), if any, are not refunded.

## 6. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Order status Packed; customer wants to change flat number in the same building. Allowed once.
- **Example B:** Order status Packed; customer moving from Delhi to Lucknow wants delivery in Lucknow. Different city - cancel and reorder.
- **Example C:** Order status Shipped; customer wants to change pincode. Not possible. Suggest refusing delivery and reordering, or arranging someone to receive at the original address.
- **Example D:** Tracking not updated for 3 days in a metro. Raise a courier ticket and share update within 24 hours.

## 7. Sample customer phrasings

- "Order kahan tak pahuncha?" -> Share the tracking link from My Orders.
- "Address galat daal diya, change kar do." -> Check status; allowed once before dispatch, same city.
- "Tracking update nahi ho raha." -> Raise a courier ticket.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can I change my address after the order is shipped?**
A: No. You can refuse delivery and place a new order.

**Q: Can I pick up the order from a store?**
A: Urbane Threads is online-only; there is no store pickup.

**Q: I didn't get a tracking SMS.**
A: Check My Orders on the website; tracking is available there once shipped.

**Q: Can someone else receive my order?**
A: Yes, anyone at the address can receive it. For COD orders they must pay the amount.

## 9. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-ORD-013**. Check status before answering address or cancellation questions. Do not promise exact delivery dates or times.
