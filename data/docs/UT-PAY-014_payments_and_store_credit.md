# Payments and Store Credit

Policy-ID: UT-PAY-014
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document describes the payment methods accepted on urbanethreads.in and the rules for Urbane Threads store credit. It complements the Refunds Policy (UT-REF-004) and the Shipping and COD Policy (UT-SHP-005).

## 2. Accepted payment methods

- **UPI** (any UPI app).
- **Debit and credit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express).
- **Net banking** from major Indian banks.
- **Wallets** supported by our payment gateway.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: COD fee **₹49** per order; **not available on orders above ₹15,000** (UT-SHP-005).
- **Store credit** from the Urbane Threads wallet, alone or combined with another prepaid method.

EMI is available on eligible credit cards for orders above ₹3,000, as offered by the issuing bank. Urbane Threads does not offer its own credit or Buy Now Pay Later.

All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). International cards may be declined by the gateway.

## 3. Store credit

### 3.1 How store credit is earned

- **COD refunds:** COD customers who choose store credit instead of NEFT get a **5% bonus** on the refund amount (UT-REF-004).
- **Exchange differences:** when the new product costs less, the difference is issued as store credit (UT-EXC-002).
- **Warranty:** when a model is unavailable for replacement, store credit for the price paid may be issued (UT-WAR-007).
- **Self-ship reimbursement:** up to ₹100 for returns from non-serviceable pincodes (UT-RET-001).

### 3.2 Store credit rules

- Issued within **24 hours** of approval (for COD refunds, within 24 hours of QC pass).
- Valid for **12 months** from the date of issue.
- Usable only on **urbanethreads.in**, not on Amazon or Flipkart.
- Can be combined with coupons and other prepaid payment methods.
- **Cannot be converted to cash** or transferred to a bank account.
- Cannot be transferred to another customer account.
- If an order paid with store credit is returned, the store credit portion is restored to the wallet.

## 4. Worked examples

- **Example A:** COD refund of ₹1,500. Store credit option gives ₹1,500 + ₹75 (5%) = **₹1,575**. NEFT option gives ₹1,500.
- **Example B:** COD refund of ₹3,200 as store credit: ₹3,200 + ₹160 = **₹3,360**.
- **Example C:** Customer with ₹500 store credit buys a ₹1,299 shirt. Pays ₹799 by UPI. Free shipping applies as the order value is above ₹999.
- **Example D:** Prepaid card order refund. The 5% bonus does not apply, because prepaid refunds go to the original source (UT-REF-004). Store credit is not offered for prepaid refunds.
- **Example E:** Customer asks to withdraw ₹2,000 store credit to their bank. Not allowed.

## 5. Payment failures

- If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, the gateway auto-reverses the amount to the source within 5-7 business days.
- Customers should not place a duplicate COD order for the same items while a failed payment is reversing, to avoid double delivery.
- If no reversal arrives after 7 business days, the customer should email support@urbanethreads.in with the transaction reference.

## 6. Sample customer phrasings

- "Store credit lene pe kitna extra milega?" -> 5% bonus on COD refunds.
- "Paise kat gaye par order nahi hua." -> Auto-reversal within 5-7 business days.
- "Wallet balance bank mein transfer kar do." -> Store credit cannot be converted to cash.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is the 5% bonus available on prepaid refunds?**
A: No. The 5% store credit bonus applies only to COD refunds.

**Q: Does store credit expire?**
A: Yes, 12 months from issue.

**Q: Can I pay part COD and part store credit?**
A: No. Store credit can only be combined with prepaid methods.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-PAY-014** for payment method and store credit questions, and UT-REF-004 for refund timelines. Always calculate the 5% bonus accurately when quoting store credit amounts.
