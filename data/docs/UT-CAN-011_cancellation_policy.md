# Order Cancellation Policy

Policy-ID: UT-CAN-011
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Operations and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when and how a customer can cancel an order placed on urbanethreads.in, and what happens to the payment. Amazon and Flipkart orders must be cancelled on the marketplace (UT-MKT-008); brand support cannot cancel them.

## 2. The core rule

- **Before dispatch: cancellation is free.** The full amount, including shipping charge (₹79) and COD fee (₹49) if any, is refunded as per UT-REF-004 (prepaid: original source in 5-7 business days; COD orders have nothing to refund unless paid in advance).
- **After dispatch: the order cannot be cancelled.** The customer has two options:
  1. **Refuse the delivery** when the courier arrives. The package returns to our warehouse and the refund is processed after it is received (prepaid orders). Shipping (₹79) and COD fee (₹49) are not refunded when a dispatched order is refused.
  2. **Accept the delivery and use the return flow** within 7 days of delivery (UT-RET-001), subject to return eligibility. Non-returnable items (innerwear, socks, Final Sale - UT-NRT-003) cannot be returned, so refusing delivery is the better option for them if the customer no longer wants the item.

"Dispatched" means the order status shows **Shipped** and a tracking number (AWB) has been generated.

## 3. How to cancel before dispatch

1. Go to **My Orders** on urbanethreads.in.
2. Select the order or item and tap **Cancel**.
3. Choose a reason and confirm.
4. A confirmation email is sent immediately. Prepaid refunds are initiated at once.

If the Cancel button is not visible, the order has already been dispatched.

## 4. Partial cancellation

Individual items from a multi-item order can be cancelled before dispatch. If cancellation reduces the remaining order value to ₹999 or below, the ₹79 shipping charge is **not** retrospectively added; the free shipping already granted is kept.

## 5. Cancellations by Urbane Threads

We may cancel an order if the item goes out of stock after ordering, the pincode becomes non-serviceable, or the order is flagged for suspected fraud. In such cases, the full amount is refunded to the original payment source in 5-7 business days.

## 6. Worked examples

- **Example A:** Order placed at 10 AM, customer cancels at 11 AM, status "Confirmed". Free cancellation; full refund to original source.
- **Example B:** Order shows "Shipped" with AWB. Customer wants to cancel. Explain that it cannot be cancelled now; they can refuse delivery or return it within 7 days of delivery.
- **Example C:** Order of a Final Sale dress already shipped. Customer changed mind. Final Sale cannot be returned, so advise refusing delivery.
- **Example D:** Customer forgot to add GSTIN and the order is not yet dispatched. They can cancel for free and reorder with GSTIN (UT-GST-010).
- **Example E:** Customer wants to cancel a ₹28,000 order that is already shipped and threatens a chargeback. Escalate to a human agent (UT-ESC-012).

## 7. Sample customer phrasings

- "Order cancel karna hai, abhi ship nahi hua." -> Free cancellation via My Orders.
- "Ship ho gaya hai, ab cancel ho sakta hai?" -> No; refuse delivery or use the return flow.
- "Delivery boy aaye toh mana kar doon?" -> Yes, refusing delivery is allowed.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is there a cancellation fee?**
A: No, cancellation before dispatch is free.

**Q: Can I cancel after the order is out for delivery?**
A: No, but you can refuse the delivery.

**Q: My COD order was cancelled. Do I get anything back?**
A: COD orders are paid at delivery, so nothing is charged if cancelled before dispatch.

**Q: Can you stop the courier for me?**
A: We cannot recall a shipment in transit. Please refuse it at the door.

## 9. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-CAN-011**. Check order status first. Never promise to cancel a dispatched order; offer refuse-delivery or return-flow options clearly.
