# Marketplace Orders (Amazon and Flipkart)

Policy-ID: UT-MKT-008
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Marketplace Operations and Customer Experience, Urbane Threads

## 1. Purpose and scope

Urbane Threads products are sold on our own website (urbanethreads.in) and on two marketplaces: **Amazon** and **Flipkart**. This policy explains what brand support can and cannot do for orders placed on these marketplaces. It is one of the most frequently misapplied policies, so agents should read it carefully.

## 2. The core rule

- **Returns, exchanges, refunds and cancellations for Amazon and Flipkart orders follow the marketplace's own process.**
- **Urbane Threads support cannot issue refunds, process returns, approve replacements, or cancel marketplace orders.** The marketplace holds the payment and controls the order.
- Agents must **redirect** the customer to the relevant marketplace: the "Your Orders" section on Amazon, or "My Orders" on Flipkart, or the marketplace's customer support.

Our own policies (7-day returns, 15-day exchanges, NEFT/store credit refunds) do **not** apply to marketplace orders. The marketplace's return window may differ and is shown on the marketplace product page.

## 3. How to identify a marketplace order

- Amazon order IDs look like **403-1234567-1234567** (3-7-7 digits).
- Flipkart order IDs start with **OD** followed by digits, e.g. **OD12345678901234567**.
- Website order IDs start with **UT** followed by digits, e.g. **UT2026051234**.
- The customer's invoice will show "Sold by" the marketplace seller entity.

If the customer is unsure, ask where they placed the order before giving any policy answer.

## 4. What brand support CAN do for marketplace customers

- Share product information: size guide, fabric, wash care.
- Explain our 6-month watch and sunglasses warranty terms (UT-WAR-007) and help with technical assessment, while directing the claim to start on the marketplace.
- Share general information about our products and website offers.
- Escalate to a human if the customer raises a legal threat or is abusive (UT-ESC-012).

## 5. What brand support must NOT do

- Must not say "we will refund you directly" or "we'll process your refund".
- Must not offer store credit, coupons or bank transfers as compensation for a marketplace order.
- Must not promise that the marketplace will approve a return.
- Must not ask for bank details for a marketplace order.

## 6. Worked examples

- **Example A:** "I bought a shirt on Amazon and it doesn't fit. Can you refund me?" -> "Since this order was placed on Amazon, the return and refund are handled by Amazon. Please go to Your Orders on Amazon and select Return or Replace Items. (Policy UT-MKT-008)"
- **Example B:** "Flipkart rejected my return. Please refund me yourselves." -> Empathise, explain that Urbane Threads cannot refund Flipkart orders, and suggest contacting Flipkart support to dispute the decision. If the customer threatens legal action, escalate (UT-ESC-012).
- **Example C:** "My Amazon order is late." -> Delivery is handled by Amazon; please check tracking in the Amazon app.
- **Example D:** "I got a damaged kurta from Flipkart." -> Raise the damage claim on Flipkart; their return policy applies. Our 48-hour damaged item process (UT-DMG-009) is for website orders.

## 7. Sample customer phrasings (Hinglish)

- "Amazon se liya tha, aap refund kar do na." -> Redirect to Amazon.
- "Flipkart wala order cancel karna hai." -> Cancel from Flipkart My Orders.
- "Amazon pe return window khatam ho gayi, aap le lo." -> We cannot accept returns on marketplace orders.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can I exchange my Flipkart purchase on urbanethreads.in?**
A: No. Exchanges for Flipkart orders must be raised on Flipkart.

**Q: Can I get a GST invoice for my Amazon order from you?**
A: The marketplace provides the invoice; download it from your Amazon order page.

**Q: Will my website store credit work on Amazon?**
A: No. Store credit works only on urbanethreads.in.

## 9. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-MKT-008**. Identify the channel first. Keep the redirect short, polite and specific about where the customer should go. Never promise any refund for a marketplace order.
