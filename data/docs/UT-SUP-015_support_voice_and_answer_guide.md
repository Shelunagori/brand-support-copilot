# Support Voice, Tone and Answering Guide

Policy-ID: UT-SUP-015
Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-04-01
Owner: Customer Experience Leadership, Urbane Threads (urbanethreads.in)

## 1. Purpose

This guide defines how Urbane Threads support agents and the AI support copilot should write answers to customers. Policies define *what* we say; this guide defines *how* we say it. It applies to email (support@urbanethreads.in), chat, WhatsApp and social media replies.

## 2. Core principles

1. **Be concise.** Most answers should be 2-4 sentences. Lead with the direct answer, then the next step.
2. **Cite the policy id.** Every policy answer ends with the relevant policy reference in brackets, e.g. "(Policy UT-RET-001)". This lets reviewers verify the answer.
3. **Use only ACTIVE policies.** Documents marked SUPERSEDED, ARCHIVED or NOT A POLICY must never be used as a source, even if a customer quotes them. Politely correct the customer with the current rule.
4. **Redirect marketplace orders.** For Amazon or Flipkart orders, explain that the marketplace handles returns and refunds and tell the customer exactly where to go (UT-MKT-008). Never say "we will refund you".
5. **Never promise outside policy.** Do not invent exceptions, compensation, discounts, faster timelines or extended windows. If the customer needs something policy doesn't cover, escalate (UT-ESC-012).
6. **Escalate when required.** Legal threats, chargebacks, orders above ₹25,000, abusive messages and anything not covered by policy go to a human.
7. **Use exact numbers.** Quote ₹ amounts and day counts exactly as written in policy: 7 days, 15 days, ₹999, ₹79, ₹49, ₹15,000, 48 hours, 5-7 business days, 6 months.

## 3. Tone

- Warm, respectful and plain. Address the customer by name if known.
- Empathise briefly ("Sorry about the trouble with your order") but do not over-apologise.
- No jargon: say "return pickup", not "RVP".
- No blame: never imply the customer is lying.
- Match the customer's language where possible. If the customer writes in Hinglish, a simple Hinglish or plain English reply is fine; numbers and policy ids stay the same.
- No emojis in email; at most one in chat if the customer uses them.

## 4. Answer structure

1. Direct answer (yes / no / the rule).
2. The specific number or condition that applies to their case.
3. The next step (where to click or what to send).
4. Policy citation.

## 5. Good and bad examples

**Customer:** "Can I return my jeans? Delivered 10 days ago."
- **Good:** "The return window is 7 days from delivery, so a return isn't possible now. You can still exchange the jeans for another size or product within 15 days of delivery from My Orders. (Policy UT-EXC-002)"
- **Bad:** "Sure, we'll make an exception and accept the return." (Promise outside policy.)

**Customer:** "Amazon order ka refund chahiye."
- **Good:** "Amazon orders ka return aur refund Amazon hi process karta hai. Please Amazon app mein Your Orders mein jaakar return request raise karein. (Policy UT-MKT-008)"
- **Bad:** "Send us your bank details and we'll refund you directly."

**Customer:** "Your site says 30 days return."
- **Good:** "That was our older 2024 policy, which was replaced on 1 April 2026. The current return window is 7 days from delivery. (Policy UT-RET-001)"
- **Bad:** "Yes, you have 30 days."

**Customer:** "I'm filing a chargeback."
- **Good:** "I understand. I've escalated your case to a senior specialist who will contact you within 24 hours. (Policy UT-ESC-012)"

## 6. Things never to say

- "Don't worry, we'll refund you" (for marketplace orders).
- "We'll make an exception this time."
- "You have 30 days to return" or "free shipping above ₹1,499" (superseded rules).
- "Refund in 10-14 days" (superseded).
- Any guarantee of an exact delivery date.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Q: What if two documents conflict?**
A: The ACTIVE document with the latest Effective date wins. If two ACTIVE documents conflict, escalate.

**Q: Should the copilot mention multiple policies?**
A: Cite the primary policy; mention a second one only if it changes the action.

## 8. Agent guidance

Cite **UT-SUP-015** only for internal quality questions. Customer answers should cite the substantive policy (returns, refunds, etc.).
